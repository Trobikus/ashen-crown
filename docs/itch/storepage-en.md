## Description

```markdown
You wake in the ash. Combat runs itself — the decisions are yours.

**Ashen Crown** is a dark-fantasy idle RPG: hunt creatures, gather ash, forge your hero — and descend area by area until, at the end of the void, the Ashen Crown itself rises to meet you.

| 🌍 | 🗺️ | ⚔️ | 🌳 | 🏆 | 🌌 |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **5** continents | **50** areas | **163** creatures | **217** skill nodes | **29** achievements | **∞** depths |

### Systems that interlock

- **⚔️ Combat & zones** — Every area carries its own bestiary of three creatures; every boss tenth waits behind 30 victories. Bosses enrage below 35 % health, every zone fights with its own temperament, and every enemy wears the color of its continent.
- **🌳 The skill tree** — 217 nodes in the Path of Exile tradition: four paths, twelve keystones with real trade-offs, twelve bridges for build ideas that exist exactly once. It awakens at level 20 — and it demands commitment: at most two paths at a time.
- **🗡️ Loot & inventory** — Seven slots, five rarities, 35 item bases. Every piece rolls its own values (hold Alt to see the ranges) — the perfect exemplar is a find, not a calculation. Drag & drop included.
- **🏺 The workshop** — Reinforce gear, reforge rarities, re-roll golden masteries: the endgame hunt for the perfect roll.
- **🏆 Glory & achievements** — 29 awards pay out Glory, and the Glory shop sells permanent conveniences: more inventory, lower death toll, longer offline progress.
- **👑 Crown Ash** — Past the crown begins the meta layer: awaken again, gather Crown Ash, unlock legacy titles — and climb deeper than ever before.

### Good to know

- **Language:** German and English — the game follows your system language and switches live, any time. Everything is translated: UI, names, lore, epitaphs, number formats.
- **Platform:** Playable **in the browser** or as a **Windows download** (updates itself). macOS and Linux builds are on the way.
- **Saves:** Export/import to file is built in (options → game). In the browser, strict privacy settings can block local storage — for long runs, the desktop build is the safer home.
- **Offline progress:** Your hero keeps fighting for up to 8 hours while you're away (20 with Depth Lore).
- **Alpha:** Closed alpha with regular updates — feedback welcome, in the comments here or on GitHub.

*The ash remembers. The throne waits.*
```
